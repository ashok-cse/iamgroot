# Job portals by country

Where to look for roles, country by country. The list covers the countries candidates have asked for so far; add a section whenever a new country is used.

## How to use a portal

- **Portals are for finding. Apply on the employer's own career page** whenever the posting links to one: the form is shorter, it needs no account, and the application lands in the employer's own system.
- **Most portals need an account to apply or to see full results.** The agent never creates an account or signs in. The candidate creates the accounts they want and signs in themselves; the agent can then search inside a signed-in browser session.
- **Open feed** in the tables below means the portal answers an anonymous request with JSON or RSS, so listings can be read without a browser or an account. Send nothing about the candidate in those requests.
- A role found on a portal is checked against the tracker by company and title, not by URL: the same role appears under many URLs.
- Portals show whatever the employer typed. Check sponsorship, language and location on the employer's own posting before applying.

## Company career pages (every country)

Most technology employers publish their openings through an applicant-tracking system. `iamgroot.py discover` reads seven of them from a list of company names: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Personio, Recruitee and Workable. Other systems seen often: Workday and iCIMS (large companies; account required), SAP SuccessFactors, Teamtailor (very common in Sweden and Finland), JOIN and Personio (Germany, Switzerland, Austria), BambooHR, Pinpoint and Breezy.

## Worldwide

| Portal | Address | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn Jobs | linkedin.com/jobs | Largest board in every country here. Account needed; "Easy Apply" runs inside the candidate's login. |
| Indeed | indeed.com and country sites | Aggregator with a site per country. Account needed to apply. |
| Glassdoor | glassdoor.com | Listings with pay data and employer reviews. |
| Wellfound | wellfound.com | Startups. Account needed. |
| Work at a Startup | workatastartup.com | Y Combinator companies. Account needed. |

| Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta) | welcometothejungle.com | Technology roles in the UK, the US and Europe. Account needed. |
| Relocate.me | relocate.me | Roles that come with relocation or visa support. |
| Levels.fyi | levels.fyi/jobs | Listings next to verified pay data; useful for salary research. |
| Remote OK | remoteok.com | Remote roles. Open feed: `remoteok.com/api`. |
| Remotive | remotive.com | Remote roles. Open feed: `remotive.com/api/remote-jobs`. |
| Himalayas | himalayas.app | Remote roles with the countries each employer hires in. Open feed: `himalayas.app/jobs/api`. |
| We Work Remotely | weworkremotely.com | Remote roles. RSS feeds per category. |

Remote roles still have a hiring country. Check the list of countries the employer can employ in before treating "remote" as open to the candidate.

## Adding a country

For a new country record: the public employment service, the two or three largest general boards, the technology or field-specific boards, the boards for people who do not speak the local language, the startup board, and the official list of employers allowed to sponsor work permits if the country publishes one. Test whether each offers an open feed and note it here.
